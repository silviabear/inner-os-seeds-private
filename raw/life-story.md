I was a bit of a strange kid. I don’t know if it’s because my father‘s departure from my life when I was three years old. He was leaving to work in Singapore. I remembered the car that took him away - it’s a police car, appropriated by my uncle who worked in the government. I remembered running after the car. I didn’t remember if I fall on the ground or not - even if it didn’t happen in real life, it felt like it happened metaphorically, that I was just exhausted, helpless, and defeated on the ground. I told my fellow kids in the kindergarten that my absent dad was a police. And when my mom asked me why, I told her that I didn’t want people to endanger us.
A lot of my time in kindergarten I was in my own world. I remembered going around the kids in my kindergarten pretending I was a butterfly and going up to different kids. I didn’t remember what their reactions were like, maybe indifference. The same thing played out nicely when I was in middle school, I was nice and warm and approachable and people welcomed me, despite some of my insensitivity. Unfortunately, it played out in the opposite way during high school. My approach and invitation to play was interpreted as absolute weirdness, and I was severely bullied. The worse part of it wasn’t the obvious abuse, but what’s unsaid. I was clueless for a very long time, something was off and I was depressed and miserable but I couldn’t name it. Until one day my cousin in another class told me that she met someone from my class (it was a 600 people class, and I had no idea what that person was) told her just offhandedly that there was this “super weird and notorious person in the class” - which was me. That’s the time I realized I was shun. Obviously I was said to show off my sexiness to seduce guys all the time, which I had no clue of. Up until today, there were still part of it that looked like a mystery to me as to what happened. My partner always said that he felt the bullying was not coming out of nowhere, it might actually come out of something special in me - which was a good thing.
