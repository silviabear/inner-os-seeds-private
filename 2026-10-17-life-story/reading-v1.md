approaching manner is described as working in middle school — "nice and

failing sharply in high school, where it was "interpreted as absolute
weirdness" and led to severe bullying. As before, the piece is explicit
that the harder part was "what's unsaid": a long period of sensing
something was wrong without being able to name it, resolved only when a
cousin reported, secondhand, an acquaintance's offhand description of her
as "weird and notorious." The text closes, as before, on a second,
confusing accusation she says she had no awareness of, an unresolved
"mystery," and her partner's reframe — that the bullying came from
"something special" in her.
